# Practice #1 - Monday Nov 23 

#### 3:00-3:20 Warmup Game: One-life or Dodgeball (20 min)

#### 3:20-3:30 Advanced Tumbling (10 min)
  - Run in a circle and do the tumbling drill on one long side
    - Forward Somersault
    - Superman roll
    - Backward Somersault
    - Cartwheels
    - Roundoffs
    - Duck-walk
    - Shot-stop

#### 3:30-3:40 Stretching (10 min)

#### 3:40-3:45 Stance and Motion (5 min)

#### 3:45-4:00 - Talk (15 min)
  - Practice expectations.
    - Listen during instruction.
    - RESPECT, 
      - Be a good partner
      - Help each other when we practice
      - Go hard when we go live
    - New kids - really pay attention
      - We will come around and help and catch you up as we can
  - Explain basic wrestling match 
    - The mat 
    - Up/Down/Neutral positions 
    - Periods and overtime 
    - Rules (stalling/clasping/fullnelson/striking/biting, ect)

#### 4:00-4:05 - Show Move (Double Leg) (5 min)
  - GOOD stance, 3 H's Head/Hands/Hips Defense
  - **Level Change!!** 
  - Clear hands
  - and penetrate
  - Step inside
  - Head outside
  - Trail leg steps up
  - Drive to finish sideways
  - **Always** land on top, not parallel

#### 4:05-4:15 - DRILL Practice double leg (10 min)
  - **GO SLOW** - focus on perfect execution

#### 5 MINUTE WATER BREAK

#### 4:20-4:25 - Show Move (Double Leg Defense) (5 min)

  - Basic go-behind from stuffing head 
  - Reaction time and hips are key

#### 4:25-4:35 - DRILL Practice double leg defense (10 min) 
  - **GO SLOW** - focus on perfect execution
