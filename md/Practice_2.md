# Practice #2 - Tuesday Nov 24 - Weight Certification Day

#### 3:00-3:40 Game: One-life or Dodgeball
  - Kids play dodgeball or one-life
  - Individuals are called out one by one or grade by grade to get certified

#### 3:40-3:50 Advanced Tumbling **(10 min)**
  - Run in a circle and do the tumbling drill on one long side
    - Shot-step
    - Cartwheels
    - Forward roll
    - Back roll
    - ect

#### 3:50-4:00 Stretching (10 min)

#### 4:00-4:05 - Refresh (Double Leg) (5 min)
  - GOOD stance, 3 H's Head/Hands/Hips Defense
  - **Level Change!!** 
  - Clear hands
  - and penetrate
  - Step inside
  - Head outside
  - Trail leg steps up
  - Drive to finish sideways
  - **Always** land on top, not parallel

#### 4:05-4:15 - DRILL Practice double leg (10 min)
  - **GO SLOW** - focus on perfect execution

#### 5 MINUTE WATER BREAK

#### 4:20-4:25 - Refresh (Double Leg Defense) (5 min)
  - Sprawl, hips in, laces to the mat, stuff head
  - Basic go-behind from stuffing head 
  - Add cross-face, show cross-face technique, don't break noses

#### 4:25-4:35 - DRILL Practice double leg defense (10 min) 
  - **GO SLOW** - focus on perfect execution
  - Practice both head-stuff and cross-face

#### 4:35-4:40 - Talk (5 min)
  - Explain power-half from head-stuff, don't drill, just introduce

#### 4:40-4:50 LIVE - 30s Live Go (10 min)
  - Wrestler A starts with double leg, Wrestler B defends
  - USE GROUPS OF 3 IF NOT ENOUGH MAT SPACE!
  - If groups of 3:
    - [ ] 1 & 2
    - [ ] 1 & 2
    - [ ] 1 & 3
    - [ ] 1 & 3
    - [ ] 2 & 1
    - [ ] 2 & 3
    - [ ] 2 & 1
    - [ ] 2 & 3
    - [ ] 3 & 1
    - [ ] 3 & 2
    - [ ] 3 & 1
    - [ ] 3 & 2
  - On coaches whistle

#### 4:50-5:00 Conditioning (10 min)
  - 30 second drills
  - Team push-up and holds
  - Partner sit-ups
  - Partner leapfrogs (Aaron to demonstrate)
  - Lunges
  - Extended leg holds
  - WWKD? (What would Kenny do)

#### 5:00-5:10 Game (one-life) (10 min)

#### 5:10-5:15 Post-practice Coach Andy Talk (5 min)
  - Weight certs reminder
  - Showering reminder
