#### 3:45-4:00 - Talk (15 min)
  - Reminder: Practice expectations
    - Listen during instruction
    - Experienced wrestlers should not ignore fundamentals
    - Be a good partner
    - Drilling = Slow and methodical
    - Practice does not make perfect, perfect practice makes perfect
    - Grab a coach when you need help but keep working
    - Injury Policy
      - Injuries means you still show up to practice

      - You get cardio every day while rehabbing unless concussion protocol
      - You come to mat during technique teaching, then back to bike/workout
    - Academic Policy
      - If you are behind on homework, you do the homework at the table
      - next to wrestling room DURING practice
      - You do not skip practice
      - Your coaches will be checking in with you
      - and understanding what you're working on
      - and how you are getting back on track and back to practice

#### 4:00-4:05 - Show Move (Outside Single Leg) (5 min)
  - GOOD stance
  - Setup - elbow tie, side step (left, left, right) 
  - Head inside, knee outside
  - Partner is not sprawling
  - Finish: drive up, lock leg, open hand punch armpit, end on top
  - Other finishes to follow in later practices, keeping it simple now
  - **WATCH MAT SPACE**, bigs on one end, littles on the other

#### 4:05-4:15 - DRILL Practice Single Leg (10 min)
  - **GO SLOW** - focus on perfect execution + no injuries

#### 5 MINUTE WATER BREAK
