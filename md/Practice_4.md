# Practice #3 - Friday Nov 27 - Optional practice 9:00am to 11:30am
- Since it's an optional practice, not introducing new material

#### 9:00am-9:20 Warmup Game: One-life or Dodgeball (20 min)

#### 9:20-9:30 Advanced Tumbling (10 min)
  - Run in a circle and do the tumbling drill on one long side
    - Forward Somersault
    - Superman roll
    - Backward Somersault
    - Cartwheels
    - Roundoffs
    - Duck-walk
    - Shot-stop

#### 9:30-9:40 Stretching (10 min)

#### 9:40-9:45 Stance and Motion (5 min)

#### 9:45-10:00 - Talk (15 min)
  - Stalling, cautions, false starts, penality rules
  - p1: 1 point, p2: 1 point, p3: 2 points, p4: disqualified
  - Refs will be more intentional about push-out stalling this year
    - Just pushing off the mat, not letting opponent back in, can result in stalling on you
    - Best to shoot opponent off the mat

#### 10:00-10:05 - Refresh (Outside Single Leg) (5 min)
  - GOOD stance
  - Setup - elbow tie, side step (left, left, right)
  - Add head-pound setup
  - Head inside, knee outside
  - Partner is not sprawling
  - Finish: drive up, lock leg, open hand punch armpit, end on top
  - Other finishes to follow in later practices, keeping it simple now
  - **WATCH MAT SPACE**, bigs on one end, littles on the other

#### 10:05-10:15 - DRILL Practice Single Leg (10 min)
  - **GO SLOW** - focus on perfect execution + no injuries
  - Emphasize head-pound setup

#### 5 MINUTE WATER BREAK

#### 10:20-10:25 - Refresh (Single Leg Defense) (5 min)
  - 3 H's Head/Hands/Hips Defense
  - Sprawl, hips in, laces to the mat
  - Wizzer - very important!
  - Clear leg
  - Look for basic go-behind, or reset neutral
  - Partner give enough resistance to so partner gets a good feel

#### 10:25-10:35 - DRILL Practice single leg defense (10 min) 
  - **GO SLOW** - focus on perfect execution
